Compliance Framework

Compliance & Consumer Consent Infrastructure

Every consumer in our acquisition system passes through a multi-layer compliance architecture built around explicit consent, regulatory standards, and complete audit trails.

🛡️ TCPA-Conscious Outreach Systems  ·  CMS-Compliant Medicare Protocols  ·  Full DNC Filtering
📝

Explicit Opt-In Systems

Every consumer provides prior express written consent before entering our transfer pipeline. Consent language is vertically specific and legally reviewed.

🕒

Timestamp + IP Logging

All consent events are logged with timestamp, IP address, user agent, and session ID — creating a complete, exportable audit trail per consumer record.

🎙️

100% Recorded Call QA

Every pre-screen and transfer call is recorded and available for client review. QA scoring ensures consistent compliance and quality across all agents.

🚫

DNC Registry Filtering

Real-time scrubbing against federal and state Do-Not-Call registries on every single lead before any outreach is initiated.

📋

TCPA-Conscious Outreach

All contact attempts are structured around TCPA guidelines — timing windows, consent verification, and documented revocation handling protocols.

🔐

Consent-Based Data Routing

Consumer data is only routed to partners whose consent language specifically covers that consumer. No cross-vertical sharing without explicit authorization.
